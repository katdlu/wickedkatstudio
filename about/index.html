Want to get to know me better?

Hej! I'm Kat (she/they/any), your typical zillennial. Too young to for adulting, too old to be a tiktoker, so I drown my existential dread in too many hobbies. I was born in late '90 in Easter Europe and emigrated to Nordics as soon as it was legal for me to do. I'm queer, I won't be more specific about it here rn. Just know, it is safe space for everybody. For a long time I struggled to find my way and tried a lot of different educations and jobs, but now I work as a drafter in a municipality and I do pottery and other crafts on the side. This website is for me a refuge from social medias, a safe space from algorithms, a punk way to share my projects and thougths with others. I also have a garden allotment where I try to grow veggies. I'm proud mother of two cats: Giffel and Mochi.
My Big 3 is: Sun in Scorpio, Moon in Leo, Ascendant in Cancer.
